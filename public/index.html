About me

I spent four years in the Navy, working on catapults and then on pilot survival gear: parachutes, flight equipment, survival systems. I inspected and repaired it, and I trained the sailors who came in after me.

After I got out I worked retail and tech support while I learned to code. That got me to Accenture, where I spent almost two years as a Java and Spring developer on a Medicaid and WIC eligibility portal used by Texas, Ohio and Kansas.

Then I went back to school and finished my B.A.S. in Software Development this past May. Now I'm looking for a junior engineering role in Austin, somewhere I can keep learning from people further along than me.
